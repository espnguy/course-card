⊕ Target Strategy
Aim for center of green, not the pin — unless you have a stock shot to that pin
Pick a specific spot (tree branch, sprinkler, discoloration) — not "the green"
Play below the hole — uphill putts are always easier than downhill
Know your safe miss side — favor the fat side of the green away from trouble
Club for carry distance, not total yardage — especially into wind
The Rule Above All
Decide before you address the ball.
Club, target, shot shape — locked in.
No last-second changes over the ball.
"A committed bad swing beats an uncommitted good one."

Read the Grain — 3 Tells
☀
Shine test Shiny/light = down grain (fast). Dark/dull = into grain (slow).
◎
Cup edge The rough, brown, worn-down side of the cup points the direction grain grows.
↓
Default rules (Bermuda) Grain grows toward the setting sun (west) on flat greens, or downhill (where water flows).
↰ INTO Grain
  • Club will grab — expect chunks
  • Ball back, hands ahead
  • Firmer strike, short follow-through
  • Ball lands & stops fast
↱ DOWN Grain
  • Ball comes out hot
  • Use less club / more loft
  • Softer landing spot — pick short
  • Expect extra roll
"Into grain: land it there. Down grain: land it short."
