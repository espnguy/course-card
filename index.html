▲ Ball ABOVE feet
  • Stand taller
  • Choke down on grip
  • Weight balanced
  • Ball pulls LEFT → aim RIGHT
▼ Ball BELOW feet
  • Bend more at hips
  • Sit into heels, more knee flex
  • Stay down through swing
  • Ball pushes RIGHT → aim LEFT
↗ UPHILL — High
  • Shoulders match slope
  • Ball forward
  • Weight on trail (lower) foot
  • Club UP 1–2 (flies high, short)
↘ DOWNHILL — Low
  • Shoulders match slope
  • Ball back
  • Weight on lead (lower) foot
  • Club DOWN (flies low, runs)
⊕ Aim Rule
Weight favors the lower foot. Aim where the ball won't go: opposite of the curve.
"Match the slope. Aim away from it."

Read the Grain — 3 Tells
☀
Shine test Shiny/light = down grain (fast). Dark/dull = into grain (slow).
◎
Cup edge The rough, brown, worn-down side of the cup points the direction grain grows.
↓
Default rules (Bermuda) Grain grows toward the setting sun (west) on flat greens, or downhill (where water flows).
↰ INTO Grain
  • Club will grab — expect chunks
  • Ball back, hands ahead
  • Firmer strike, short follow-through
  • Ball lands & stops fast
↱ DOWN Grain
  • Ball comes out hot
  • Use less club / more loft
  • Softer landing spot — pick short
  • Expect extra roll
"Into grain: land it there. Down grain: land it short."
